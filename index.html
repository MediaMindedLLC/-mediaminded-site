AI + live transfer

AI warms the lead, your team closes

The AI does the dialing and the first few minutes. When the lead is qualified and interested, it transfers the live call to a rep with the context on their screen.

  • Reps only take connected, qualified calls
  • Spoken intro, so it feels like one conversation
  • If no one's free, the AI books instead

Most clients run both — full AI after hours, live transfer when the floor is staffed.

Common questions

How does a live transfer work for the lead?

It sounds like one call. The AI says it's bringing in a specialist, introduces the lead by name, and drops off. Your rep already sees who it is and what's been covered.

What if none of my reps are available?

The AI books an appointment or schedules a callback. No qualified lead gets dropped.

Does the AI say it's an AI?

Yes, on every call. It also honors do-not-call and opt-out requests immediately.

Will this work with our CRM and dialer?

Yes. Leads, recordings, transcripts, and appointments sync back to the tools you already use.
