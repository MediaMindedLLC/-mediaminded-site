Two ways to run it. One platform underneath.

Every deployment uses the same AI agents, call recording, lead scoring, and CRM integrations. The only question is who finishes the conversation — the AI, or one of your people.

Full AI

AI handles every customer interaction

The AI calls and texts your leads, answers questions, handles objections, qualifies interest, and books the appointment straight onto your sales calendar. No one on your side touches the phone until the estimate.

  • Outbound calling and SMS follow-up at any volume
  • Inbound answering, 24/7, with no hold queue
  • Qualification and booking with your real calendar availability
  • Confirmation and no-show reduction sequences
  • Aged-list re-engagement

Best fit: teams without a call center, high lead volumes, aged lists, or anyone who wants booked appointments without adding headcount.

AI + live transfer

AI warms the lead, then hands it to your team

The AI does the dialing, the waiting, and the first few minutes of every conversation. When a lead is engaged and qualified, it transfers the live call to one of your reps with a spoken intro and the full context on their screen. Your people only talk to people who want to talk.

  • Reps receive only connected, qualified, interested calls
  • Warm introduction so the handoff feels like one conversation
  • Context pushed to the rep: name, job, timeline, objections already covered
  • Routing by skill, territory, or availability; fallback to booking if no one is free
  • Works with your existing dialer or phone system

Best fit: call centers and sales floors that want to keep closers on the phone, cut dial time, and protect their talk-time for conversations that matter.

Most clients end up with a mix

Full AI overnight and on weekends, live transfer during floor hours. Full AI on aged lists, live transfer on fresh web leads. The rules are yours to set, and you can change them any time without a rebuild.

Questions we hear on every discovery call

What's the difference between the two models?

In Full AI, the AI handles the entire conversation and books the appointment itself. In AI + live transfer, the AI does the dialing and the first part of the conversation, then transfers qualified, interested callers to one of your reps in real time. You can run either, or both with rules for when each applies.

How does a live transfer actually work for the lead?

It sounds like one continuous call. The AI says it's bringing in a specialist, briefly introduces the lead by name to your rep, and drops off. Your rep's screen already shows who it is, what they need, and what's been covered, so nobody repeats themselves.

What happens if none of my reps are available?

You choose the fallback: the AI books an appointment on your calendar, schedules a callback at a time the lead picks, or sends the lead to a voicemail or text queue. No qualified lead gets dropped because the floor was busy.

Does the AI tell people it's an AI?

Yes, on every call. It also honors do-not-call and opt-out requests immediately and only dials inside permitted calling windows for the lead's time zone.

Will this work with our CRM and dialer?

Yes. The platform runs alongside the tools you already use. Leads, call recordings, transcripts, dispositions, and appointments sync back to your system of record.

How fast can we go live?

Most Full AI deployments are live in 3–5 business days after the discovery call. Live-transfer setups take about the same, plus whatever time your phone system or dialer needs on your side.

How many leads do we need?

For the performance model we recommend at least 500 leads to start, though we've worked lists from 200 to 50,000+. Live-transfer deployments are scoped to your call volume and seat count instead.
